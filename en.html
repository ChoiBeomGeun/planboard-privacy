Privacy Policy

Workout Planboard · Effective 2026-09-27

Workout Planboard (the "App") handles your personal information with care, as described below.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information (optional) — only when you sign in for cloud sync (Pro): your email address and social sign-in identifier (ID provided by Google/Kakao/Apple)
  • Workout data — the workouts, weekly plan, wake-up/work time settings and daily records you enter
  • Lock Screen alert data (when checkpoint alerts are on) — the push token issued by Apple, a random device identifier created by the App, and today's and tomorrow's Lock Screen schedule (times, task names, short messages). It contains no identity information such as your name or email.
  • Update checks — app version information (runtime version, current update ID) when the App checks for a new version

If you don't sign in, your workout data is stored only on your device. However, when checkpoint alerts are on, the Lock Screen alert data above is sent to a server run by the developer so the Lock Screen board can update on time even when the App isn't open.

2. How we use it

  • Identifying your account and keeping you signed in
  • Syncing workout data across devices (signed-in users only)
  • Sending updates that change the Lock Screen board (Live Activity) at the scheduled times — via the Apple Push Notification service (APNs)
  • Delivering app updates

We do not collect or use data for advertising, selling profiles, or third-party marketing.

3. Storage and processors

  • Cloud sync data — stored on Supabase (supabase.com) infrastructure over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and kept until you delete your account.
  • Lock Screen alert data — stored on a server run directly by the developer (located in the Republic of Korea); alerts are delivered to your device through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Past schedule entries are deleted automatically after 2 days, and data for devices that haven't connected for 30 days is deleted automatically.
  • Server connection — the connection between the App and the server is encrypted (HTTPS) and relayed through Tailscale (tailscale.com). Ordinary connection information such as IP addresses may be processed during connection, but it is not separately stored or used for other purposes.

4. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all data stored on the server immediately and permanently at any time in the App's Settings (account and sync section). Lock Screen alert data is removed from the server as soon as you turn off checkpoint alerts in Settings, and is deleted automatically 30 days after you delete the App. Data stored on your device is removed by deleting the App or clearing browser data.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties except when required by law.
